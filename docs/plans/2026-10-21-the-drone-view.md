## The tasks

| # | Task | What the tests check |

| 2026-10-21 | 8 done | `site/src/fly/clip.ts`: a run becomes a loop from liftoff to touchdown, so both ends are near a calm hover (level, motors at hover, low over the ground), and the last and first 0.8 s ease to exactly that, so the loop joins without a jump. A run that comes down hard plays to where it fell and fades out, then back in; that's 32 of the 198, all the dead motors without P, and every landing under the chute too, since 4 m/s under a canopy isn't a landing. Long runs are sped up on a log scale, so the speed changes evenly, with the fast speed found by halving until the loop is its length; within 1.5 s of an event it plays at real time. Each kind of event counts once, at its first, since a failure flag raised every few seconds made the 30-minute battery runs loop for nearly 5 minutes; where the moments alone would overflow the loop, they speed up too. Over all 198 runs: every loop 15 to 30 s (median 20), the speed never changes more than 15% in 50 ms, and the slowest run takes 96 ms to prepare |
